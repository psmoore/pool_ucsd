Natatorium

An indoor 25-yard pool at the Main Gym complex, bright under glass walls and skylights. Recreation's adult and youth swim lessons and Swim Conditioning are here. The Natatorium has no drop-in swim; it is booked for classes, and the Tritons hold some winter workouts here.

Lane by lane

The pool

  • Six lanes × 25 yards, 7-foot-wide lanes, 80–83°F
  • Glass walls and skylights; outdoor patio and spa (12 people) to the south
  • One men's and one women's restroom; no showers
  • Six sets of four-row bleachers on deck
  • Rentable for pool parties, inner-tube water polo and dive-in movies

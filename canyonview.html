Canyonview Aquatic Center

Two outdoor, heated, Olympic-size pools across Voigt Drive from the Central Campus trolley stop: the Ronald “Sickie” Marcikic Pool and the Triton Pool. Lap swim, Masters, diving, club water polo, and the Tritons' home water.

The pools

Ronald “Sickie” Marcikic Pool

  • East side, the original pool, named in 2026 for Recreation's Masters director of 43 years
  • 20 lanes × 25 yards, 7-foot-wide lanes
  • Lanes 1–4: dive well, 15 feet deep, with two 1-meter springboards and two 3-meter platforms
  • Lanes 19–20: family lanes, 3 feet deep
  • 80–85°F · spa for 12 · north sun deck

Triton Pool

  • West side, opened 2006, home to the Tritons' meets and games
  • 50 meters × 25 yards: 8 long-course or 19 short-course lanes
  • 7 feet deep throughout · 680,000 gallons
  • Bleachers for 1,000 · deck lights for night use

Both pools have men's and women's locker rooms, showers and a weight room. Canyonview Fitness Gym is next door.

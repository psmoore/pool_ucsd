Aquatics

Recreation runs three pools on the La Jolla campus: the two 50-meter pools at Canyonview Aquatic Center and the indoor pool at the Main Gym Natatorium. Every time on these pages comes from the pools' live schedule, so when Recreation changes it, these pages change with it.

Where can I swim right now?

The pools

Canyonview Aquatic Center

3302 Voigt Dr, La Jolla, CA 92093

Two outdoor, heated, Olympic-size pools across Voigt Drive from the Central Campus trolley stop: the Ronald “Sickie” Marcikic Pool and the Triton Pool. Lap swim, Masters, diving, club water polo, and the Tritons' home water.

…

Main Gym Natatorium

2995 Scholars Ln, La Jolla, CA 92093

An indoor 25-yard pool at the Main Gym complex, bright under glass walls and skylights. Recreation's adult and youth swim lessons and Swim Conditioning are here.

…

Mission Bay Aquatic Center

1001 Santa Clara Pl, San Diego, CA 92106 · (858) 488-1000

Open water, not a pool: sailing, wakeboarding, waterskiing, surfing, stand-up paddling and kayaking on Mission Bay, run with Associated Students. Its classes book through mbaquaticcenter.com.

Who can swim

Lap swim and the spa at Canyonview are open to Recreation members, with a membership or day pass. Students' membership is included in registration fees. Classes and Masters have their own registration; see Aquatic Classes and Masters Swimming.

What this replaces

  • "Natatorium Indoor Pool: CLOSED." The Facility Hours page lists the Natatorium as closed, but four days a week it holds adult and youth swim lessons, Swim Conditioning and an intro diving class. Here the pool shows what is actually in the water.
  • Class times one click at a time. Each lesson time lives inside its own registration listing, and you have to open every offering to see its dates and pool. Here every class is on one calendar, by lane.
  • "Canyonview Pool." Registration sends divers to a "Canyonview Pool", while the facilities page names two pools, Marcikic and Triton. Here every booking names its pool.
  • Lap swim that has to move. Varsity games and meets in the Triton Pool, and club water polo in the evening, all take water during posted lap swim hours. Here lap swim shows which pool it's in on those days.
  • Four websites for one pool deck. Lap swim and Masters are on Recreation's hours page, classes on the registration site, club practices on each club's page, and home games on ucsdtritons.com. Here they are one schedule.

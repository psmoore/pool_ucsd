Clubs & Varsity

Sports Clubs and the Tritons share Canyonview's water with lap swimmers. Practice times and home events below come from the same live schedule as the pool calendars.

Men's Club Water Polo

National champions in 2022, 2023 and 2024, with A, B and C teams in the Collegiate Water Polo Association's Southwest Division. Practices start after Labor Day.

Practice: … · Instagram @ucsdmcwp

Women's Club Water Polo

An A team that finished 4th in the nation in 2025 and a B team for developing players. Drop in for a few practices to see if it's for you.

Practice: … · Instagram @ucsdwomensclubpolo

Club Swim

More than 120 members, competing in College Club Swimming and at CCS Nationals every year since 2023 (17th in 2026). Five coached swim practices a week at Canyonview, plus two lifting sessions at Spanos Athletic Performance Center.

Club Swim hasn't published its practice times. When they are entered, they appear here and on the Canyonview calendar. ucsdclubswim@gmail.com

Triathlon

More than 70 athletes racing Sprint and Olympic distances in the West Coast Collegiate Triathlon Conference. ucsdtriathlon@gmail.com

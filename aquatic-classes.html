Aquatic Classes

Swim lessons for adults and kids, Swim Conditioning and intro diving. Days and times below come from the live schedule; register on rec.ucsd.edu.

Registration opens by membership: UCSD students first, then REC members, then Session 2 offerings. Classes stay open until they fill or close.
